About Me

I am a PhD student at the Universitäts-Sternwarte München and the Max-Planck-Institut für extraterrestrische Physik, working under the supervision of Dr. Roberto Saglia. My research centres on the detection and characterisation of gaseous exoplanets — from hot Jupiters in stellar clusters to the enigmatic sub-Saturn population straddling the boundary between ice giants and gas giants.

My work draws on two complementary approaches. On the observational side, I use the MaHPS fibre-fed spectrograph at the Wendelstein Observatory to carry out precision radial-velocity surveys, searching for giant planets in environments such as open clusters where the host-star properties are well constrained. On the population side, I combine transit and RV data to study the internal structures and orbital architectures of sub-Saturn-mass planets, probing open questions about how these worlds form and whether they represent a distinct evolutionary channel from their larger Jovian cousins.
